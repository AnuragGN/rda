Typically, four actors participate in a payment card transaction.

Merchant: A merchant sells products or services to customers, and can use the Authorize.Net Payment Gateway and its API to submit payment transactions.
Customer: A customer buys products or services from merchants using a payment card from an issuing bank.
Issuing Bank: An issuing bank provides payment cards to customers and represent customers in disputes.
Acquiring Bank: An acquiring bank underwrites merchants so the merchants can accept payment cards. Acquiring banks also represent merchants in disputes.
