

1.  Click Admin.
2.  In the Property column, check that you have your new Google Analytics 4 property selected,
    then click Data Streams, then Web. Click the data stream.
3.  Under Tagging Instructions, click "Add new on-page tag" and look for "Global Site Tag (gtag.js)".
    Your Analytics page tag is the entire section of code that appears, beginning with:
    <!-- Global Site Tag (gtag.js) - Google Analytics -->
        and ending with
    </script>
4. Copy and paste your entire Analytics page tag immediately after the <head> on each page of your website.
